Deep Work means focusing completely on an important task without distractions for a long period of time to produce high-quality work and improve skills.

## Summary of Deep Work Book
2. According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?  
* Work without distractions.
* Schedule dedicated deep work time.
* Reduce social media and unnecessary notifications.
* Focus on one task at a time.
* Practice concentration regularly.
* Take proper breaks for recovery.
* Create a structured routine.
* Train the mind to avoid shallow work.

3. How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?  
I can implement deep work by keeping my phone on silent, avoiding social media during study time, and setting fixed hours for focused coding or learning. I will try to work on one task at a time, take short breaks when needed, and create a daily routine that helps me concentrate better without distractions.

## Dangers of Social Media
4. What are the dangers of social media, in brief?  
* Reduces focus and concentration.
* Increases distractions and procrastination.
* Wastes a lot of time.
* Affects mental health and increases anxiety.
* Reduces deep thinking ability.
* Creates addiction to constant notifications.
* Decreases productivity.
* Can negatively affect sleep and daily routine.
