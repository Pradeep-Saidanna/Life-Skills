# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment ?  
Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behavior that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, humiliated, or disrespected. This can include inappropriate comments, sexual jokes, staring, offensive messages, unwanted touching, asking for personal or sexual favors, sharing inappropriate images, repeated unwanted communication, bullying, or any verbal, non-verbal, physical, or online behavior of a sexual nature. Creating a hostile or intimidating work environment also comes under harassment.

2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?  
If I face or witness such behavior, I would stay calm and take the situation seriously. I would clearly communicate that the behavior is inappropriate if it feels safe to do so. I would document the incident, including time, place, messages, or witnesses if possible. I would report the matter to the appropriate authority such as HR, manager, or PoSH/Internal Complaints Committee instead of ignoring it. If someone else is affected, I would support them respectfully and encourage them to seek help and report the issue.
